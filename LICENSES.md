# Licensing boundaries

Public GitHub visibility is not a blanket license grant for all code, datasets or models. No new project-wide software license is selected in this initial publication.

- The primary dataset landing page statesCC BY4.0;preserve attribution and identify modifications when producing derivatives.
- Supplementary Figshare items are labelledCC0 in the recorded source manifest. That label does not override third-party code/model licenses embedded in an archive.
- OpenSim/OpenCap/model assets are not bundled or relicensed. Obtain them from their official sources and verify their own terms.
- Research summaries and portable scripts do not establish permission to process other private clinical data or substitute for institutional ethics review.

Model hashes and marker names are recorded solely as provenance/compatibility references;no model geometry, weights or marker XML is redistributed.
